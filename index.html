一、应用功能说明

“圆圆计时” 是一款模拟真实旋钮手感的沉浸式计时应用。支持通过旋转指针或快捷键设定倒计时，并可伴随背景音帮助用户专注。本应用坚持纯净工具属性：计时记录、自定义快捷键及皮肤均存储于用户设备本地，无需登录账号，开发者也不运营自己的服务器。播放 Apple Music 以及通过 App Store 购买时需要联网，相关通信由 Apple 处理。

二、数据收集与使用说明

  • 个人身份信息：本应用不收集任何姓名、手机号、邮箱等个人信息，也不获取设备标识符（IDFA/IMEI）。
  • 数据存储：您的计时习惯、自定义快捷键及已解锁的皮肤仅保存在本地，卸载应用后数据将一并清除。
  • 权限申请：
    1. 通知权限：仅用于计时结束时的强提醒。
    2. 后台音频：用于在计时进行时播放白噪音、您导入的音频以及 Apple Music，包括在锁屏或后台继续播放。
    3. Apple Music：仅在您选用 Apple Music 专辑并同意系统授权后，读取资料库中的歌曲并显示当前曲目封面。曲目由 Apple 提供，开发者不上传、不保存您的音乐资料库。
    我们不申请位置、通讯录、相机权限，也不申请完整相册权限。选择专辑封面时使用系统选择器，系统不会把整个相册交给本应用。

三、支付与订阅说明（EULA 补充）

本应用提供免费基础功能，并提供买断/订阅以解锁高级皮肤及音效。

  • 支付处理：所有交易由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不接触您的支付账户信息。
  • 自动续订：如果您选择订阅模式，除非您在当前计费周期结束前至少 24 小时关闭自动续订，否则订阅将自动延长。
  • 取消方式：您可随时通过 iOS 设备的“设置” -> “Apple ID” -> “订阅”来管理或取消。
  • 恢复购买：若更换设备，您可通过应用内的“恢复购买”按钮找回已购权益。

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use (EULA)

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. App Functionality

"OOTimer" is an immersive timer application that simulates the tactile feel of a real knob. It supports setting countdowns via a rotating pointer or shortcuts, with optional background audio for focus. As a pure utility tool, timer records, custom shortcuts, and skins are stored locally on the user's device. No account login is required, and the developer does not operate a server. An internet connection is used only to play Apple Music and to make App Store purchases. Those communications are handled by Apple.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Personal Information: We do NOT collect any names, phone numbers, email addresses, or device identifiers (such as IDFA/IMEI).
  • Data Storage: Your timing habits, custom shortcuts, and unlocked skins are stored locally on your device only. This data will be cleared if the app is uninstalled.
  • Permissions:
    1. Notifications: Used solely for alerts when the timer ends.
    2. Background Audio: Used while a timer is running to play white noise, audio you import, and Apple Music, including on the Lock Screen and in the background.
    3. Apple Music: Only after you select the Apple Music album and grant the system permission, the app reads songs from your library and shows the current track's artwork. The music is provided by Apple. The developer does not upload or store your music library.
    We do not request access to location, contacts, or the camera, and we do not request full photo library access. Choosing an album cover uses the system picker, which does not give this app your entire photo library.
